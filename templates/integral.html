Integral Method of Analysis

Assumes a single-reactant nth-order rate law -rA = -dCA/dt = k·CAn. The order is guessed, the rate law is integrated, and the data is tested for linearity:

  • n = 1: plot -ln(CA/CA0) vs. t — slope = k
  • n ≠ 1: plot CA1-n vs. t — slope = (n-1)k, intercept = CA01-n

The app scans orders from 0 to 3 (step 0.05) and reports the best-fitting order, including fractional orders.
