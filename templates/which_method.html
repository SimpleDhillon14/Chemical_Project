Which Method Should I Use: Integral or Differential?

Both methods fit -rA = k·CAn to your CA vs. t data, but they get there differently, and one usually suits your data better than the other:

  • Integral Method — guesses an order, integrates the rate law, and tests linearity for each candidate order (0, 1, 2, 3, ...). It works on the raw concentration data directly, so it's more forgiving of noisy, sparse, or unevenly-spaced data.
  • Differential Method — numerically differentiates CA vs. t to get the instantaneous rate at every point, then reads the order directly off a single log-log slope (fractional orders included, no guessing needed). Differentiation amplifies noise, so it needs dense, smooth, evenly-spaced data to be reliable.
