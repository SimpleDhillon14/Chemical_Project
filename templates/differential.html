Differential Method of Analysis

Directly follows CRE_Diff.pdf, Chapter 3: the slope dCA/dt is evaluated at each composition (numerically, via central differences at interior points and forward/backward differences at the endpoints — the numerical equivalent of "draw a smooth curve and read its slope").

log₁₀(-dCA/dt) = log₁₀(k) + n·log₁₀(CA)

Plotting log₁₀(-dCA/dt) vs. log₁₀(CA) gives a straight line whose slope = n (reaction order) and whose intercept = log₁₀(k) (Fig. 3.18).

Unlike the Integral Method, this method doesn't need to test order 0, 1, 2, 3... one at a time — the slope of this single log-log line is the order directly, fractional values included.
